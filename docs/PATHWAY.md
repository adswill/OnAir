Codec v3 (core/src/kaal/kaal_codec3.cpp, kaal_codec3_tables.h): port of kaal_codec_v3.py, profiles 12/16/24/32 kbit/s (240/320/480/640 bits per 20 ms frame), 26 bands, 4.5 dB energies, trained start probabilities per profile, the profile in the first two bits of every frame; bit stream and decoded PCM identical to the Python on the golden vectors (tests/kaal/gen_golden_codec3.py, gen_codec3_tables.py). PHY: the 202-symbol frame (25.25 ms per 20 ms of speech, TX 26 % slower than real time) is replaced by a 160-symbol frame (32 preamble + 120 data + 8 pilots = 20 ms) carrying a rate-matched word of 480 bits (the (640,320) LDPC shortened to k = 240 or 320 information bits, first 480 - k parity bits sent, ldpcEncodeRm/ldpcDecodeRm): 12 kbit/s is rate 1/2, 16 kbit/s rate 2/3, both exactly real time; 24 and 32 kbit/s take two words per codec frame, i.e. twice the air time (a file/recording choice). The receiver tries k = 240 and 320 and reads the profile from the decoded frame header. Sweep: period 24240 samples with four variants (up, down, fast1, fast2) = 2 bits per period carrying a 6-character base-40 callsign + 4-bit suffix class + CRC-8 (12.12 s cycle), core/src/kaal/kaal_sweepid.cpp; receiver SweepProc (per-period variant decision, exact analytic-template removal, per-variant position trackers) and SweepIdScanner (sweep-only ID reader on a worker thread, used by KaalLiveReceiver while the voice receiver is not locked). TX: VoiceTx::setBitrate/setSweepCallsign, TxRequest opt "kaal.bitrate" (default 16), TX panel combo, telemetry bitrateKbps/sweepId. VoiceTx(0) keeps the legacy v0.1 stream for the golden TX test, the receiver no longer decodes it. Notes: scratchpad/KAAL-V3-NOTES.md. Built and run with clang++ on the kaal sources only (tests kaal_codec3, kaal_rx_loopback, kaal_sweepid, kaal_tx_v3, kaal_engine and the old kaal tests); the full CMake/Rust build and the GUI were not run.

## 2026-10-21 Analogue TV on weak signals (analog-tv-029)
Line sync for weak signals: when the threshold slicer finds no line structure, the acquisition folds the signal at every possible line period (matched filter for the sync pulse, period scan, significance against the neighbouring periods, tip-highest and depth checks, 625/525 before 405/819) and the line loop then runs on a block template (32 lines added at the loop's own phase, sync pulse and both edges found in it, loop corrected from that) instead of per-line threshold crossings; broad field-sync pulses by their mean over 22 us. Clean signals (sync height over 1.8 times the noise) go back to the slicer. Picture: temporal noise reduction (AtvReceiver::setNoiseReduction, automatic by the video S/N, motion adaptive). Measured with the generator (PAL B/G, NTSC M, SECAM D/K, 10 Msps): the line lock held down to C/N 12 dB before (NTSC 8), now to 3 dB; mean luminance difference to the card at C/N 12 went from 72 to 32; tests/atv/test_atv_weak.cpp holds the numbers.

# GNSS interference detector (family 893)

Looks at a wide capture of a navigation band (L1 1575.42 MHz, L2 1227.6 MHz and others; a radio with at least 2 MHz of bandwidth and a GNSS antenna) and says whether it is clean, or holds a tone, narrowband interference, a swept chirp (a car-mounted jammer) or wideband noise-like interference. Receive only: no message is decoded and no position is computed.
**Simulated signals only**; the generator (`tests/x40/x40_gen2.h`, rendered block by block) is not reachable from the application. It detects and describes interference; it cannot say where it comes from or who sent it.

- Method: once a second 65536 samples are taken. A Welch spectrum (2048 points) gives the noise floor (20th-percentile bin, so that a chirp raising up to 80 % of the bins does not hide itself), the interference-to-noise ratio, the share of the band raised, and the width of the strongest line.
  A line under five bins wide and 15 dB over the floor is a tone. Otherwise the instantaneous frequency of the block is autocorrelated: a sawtooth sweep gives a strong return peak (at least 0.3, noise gives 0.03), its lag is the period, the 5 - 95 % range of the frequency the sweep range. Other narrow clusters are narrowband interference. A rise of the whole noise floor of more than 3 dB over the lowest value seen since the start (forgotten at 0.01 dB per second)
  is wideband noise-like interference. A class must hold for two seconds before it is logged; the end is logged as clean.
- Limits: a wideband jammer that is already on when the mode starts raises the baseline and is not seen as such (a change of the radio's gain also moves it); a chirp that covers most of the band reads a few dB under its true interference-to-noise ratio (15 dB read for 20 dB at 10 Msps with a 5.6 MHz sweep); a chirp must sweep more than 15 % of the sample rate and 0.5 MHz.
- Tested at 4 and 10 Msps: clean band; tone (-0.9 MHz, found within 10 kHz); narrowband (150 kHz wide); chirp (sweep range within 20 % and period within 1 us of the generator's); wideband noise (started 2 s into the run); the interference starting at 4 s and ending at 9 s (one interference row, one clean row); noise sweep; clean noise.
- Level sweep at 4 Msps (interference power over noise power, 4 s runs): tone found down to -3 dB (a tone puts all its power in one bin); chirp (2.8 MHz sweep) at +6, +3 and 0 dB, clean at -3 dB; wideband noise at +6, +3 and 0 dB, clean at -3 dB. Clean noise, 4 minutes (20 runs of 12 s at 2 Msps): no row.

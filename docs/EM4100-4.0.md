# EM4100 LF RFID (family 886)

Reads the 40-bit ID (8-bit version / customer code and 32-bit number) of EM4100 / EM4102 125 kHz tags from the load modulation they put on a reader's carrier. Receive only: nothing is written to or copied from a tag.
**Simulated signals only.** No recording of a real tag was available. The generator is in `tests/x40/x40_gen2.h` and is not reachable from the application.

- Frame (EM Microelectronic EM4100 data sheet): 64 bits Manchester at RF/64 (1953 bit/s; RF/32 is also read), 9 header ones, ten rows of 4 data bits plus even row parity, four even column parities, stop bit 0.
- Method: envelope of the carrier (an offset of the carrier within about 4 kHz does not matter), hysteresis slicer, level-change intervals give one or two half-bits (clock followed within 15 %), every 128 half-bits tested against the frame rules in both polarities. A chance hit needs all 25 parity and header checks to pass.
- Needs a radio that reaches 125 kHz (direct sampling or an LF capable SDR) and a coil near the reader; the tag must be powered by a reader.
- Tested: four IDs (all ones, 1, mixed), both load polarities, RF/64 and RF/32, bit clock 4 % fast and slow, carrier offset 0 / +2.5 / -2.5 kHz and 800 Hz/s drift, 20 kHz off-centre tuning, two different tags in a row.
- Noise (8 % modulation depth, carrier to noise ratio in 10 kHz): 3 of 3 reads at 60, 50, 45 and 40 dB, 0 of 3 at 35 dB. 15 minutes of carrier plus noise: 0 false tags.
- Not covered: EM4305 / T5577 writable tags, HID Prox, Indala, FDX-B (other formats), anti-collision, tag-talks-first modes.

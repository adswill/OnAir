# COSPAS-SARSAT 406 MHz
A 406 MHz distress beacon (EPIRB on a boat, ELT in an aircraft, PLB carried by a person) sends a burst of about half a second every 50 seconds: an unmodulated carrier, then 112 or 144 bits at 400 bit/s. The message holds a 15 digit hex ID that is unique to the beacon, the country, the kind of beacon, and often a position (rounded to 15 minutes in a short message, to 4 seconds in a long one). Two BCH checks protect the message; up to three damaged bits in the first field are repaired.\n\nOnAir only receives. Test beacons and self-test bursts are marked. If you think you heard a real one, tell the rescue coordination centre of your country; do not rely on this program for safety. Wiadomość zawiera 15-cyfrowy identyfikator szesnastkowy, unikalny dla radiopławy, kraj, rodzaj radiopławy i często pozycję (w krótkiej wiadomości zaokrągloną do 15 minut, w długiej do 4 sekund).

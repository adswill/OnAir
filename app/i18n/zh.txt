# COSPAS-SARSAT 406 MHz
A 406 MHz distress beacon (EPIRB on a boat, ELT in an aircraft, PLB carried by a person) sends a burst of about half a second every 50 seconds: an unmodulated carrier, then 112 or 144 bits at 400 bit/s.

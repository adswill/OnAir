IQ correction	Correzione IQ
Corrects I/Q gain and phase imbalance, which puts a faint mirror image of every signal on the other side of the centre.\nMeasured from the signal itself; now %.2f dB, %.1f degrees. Not applied in DTMB and analog TV (their signals fool the measurement).	Corregge lo squilibrio di guadagno e fase I/Q, che mette un'immagine speculare debole di ogni segnale dall'altra parte del centro.\nMisurata dal segnale stesso; ora %.2f dB, %.1f gradi. Non applicata in DTMB e TV analogica (i loro segnali ingannano la misura).
The spike in the middle of the spectrum is the radio's own oscillator leaking in (strong on the HackRF and RTL-SDR).\nRemove: filtered out of the samples (not in FM: a station tuned to the centre has its carrier there).\nTune beside it: the radio is tuned just beside the channel, so the spike falls outside it, and the channel is moved\nback digitally. The message holds a 15 digit hex ID that is unique to the beacon, the country, the kind of beacon, and often a position (rounded to 15 minutes in a short message, to 4 seconds in a long one). Two BCH checks protect the message; up to three damaged bits in the first field are repaired.\n\nOnAir only receives. Test beacons and self-test bursts are marked. A satellite beacon is a carrier, sometimes phase modulated with telemetry, that ground stations use to measure the link. The carrier is followed in frequency and level; the telemetry is recovered as raw frames and the frame length is learned from their repetition.

What this means for you
Digital voice (DMR, P25, NXDN, dPMR, D-STAR, Yaesu, TETRA) is silent until you install its codec plug-in. The calls, IDs and data work without it.
Only digital voice modes need a codec. You can skip this screen and come back from Help > Voice codecs.
OnAir does not include or download these codecs (they are patented). You build or install them on your own computer; whether you may use them is your own responsibility. FreeDV, M17, DAB, DRM and TV need nothing.

Easy mode (recommended)
Every mode opens its Simple view: the spectrum and one screen with a Start button and the key controls. The detailed tabs are one click away (Simple | Advanced above them).
Change any of this at any time: Help > Setup and lessons > Run setup again, Mode > Modes shown, View > Language, Settings > Appearance > Font look.
The setup has six short screens and every one can be skipped. Nothing is forced.\n\nRadio: OnAir looks for radios on USB and on the network and picks one. "?" beside a radio opens its help page. Without a radio, the test signal lets you try OnAir.\n\nLocation: one click on a region preset (Lithuania, the EU, ...) or a city, a locator or coordinates give your country, the band plan, the TV channels, the FM band and step, the default frequencies and the units.\n\nAntenna: hints for what you can hear with the antenna you have.\n\nInterests: decide which modes are starred and listed first, where OnAir starts and a few starter presets. Everything stays available.\n\nCodecs: digital voice needs a codec plug-in that you install yourself; Look again checks the folder.\n\nEasy mode on the last screen opens every mode in its Simple view. Run the setup again any time from Help > Setup and lessons > Run setup again, or from Settings.

    How to listen

    1. Click a station on the spectrum or the waterfall to tune to it, or press Enter and type a frequency in MHz, for example 98.3. Scroll to zoom, drag to move along the band.
    2. Choose the mode that matches the signal: NFM for most voice radio, WFM for FM broadcast, AM for airband and shortwave, USB or LSB for amateur voice, CW for Morse. Drag the edges of the shaded passband, or use the Bandwidth slider, to fit the signal.
    3. Press Listen. Your browser only plays sound after you press it. Use Volume and Mute as needed.
    4. Squelch keeps the audio quiet until a signal is stronger than the level you set. Move it right until the hiss stops. Fully left means open.
    5. Bands jump to a whole band. Saved keeps your own frequencies on this device: Save current stores the frequency, mode and bandwidth.

    What the messages mean

    Connecting to the receiver.
    The page is reaching the receiver. Nothing is wrong yet.
    Connected. Waiting for the first spectrum.
    You are in; the first picture arrives within a second or two.
    Offline. Retrying in N s.
    The connection dropped. The page tries again by itself.
    busy
    The receiver has all its listener places taken. It keeps retrying.
    sign in
    This receiver needs a password or an invite code.
    No receiver here.
    This address is a web page only, with no OnAir receiver behind it.
    Moved to the nearest allowed frequency.
    The host only allows some ranges. Allowed ranges are listed above the spectrum.
    dB spectrum / dBFS
    The signal meter is relative to the receiver's full scale, not calibrated. Without audio it shows the spectrum level.

# OnAir name and logo

"OnAir", the OnAir logo and the on-air.dev name are (c) 2026 Kristijonas Adomaitis. All rights reserved. The GPL that covers OnAir's source code does not license the name or the logo (see also [assets/branding](assets/branding/README.md)).

Nothing here limits the rights the GPL gives you over the code: you may run, study, modify and share it, including under another name.

## You may, without asking

- Say truthfully where your work comes from, in plain text: "based on OnAir", "a fork of OnAir", "works with OnAir", with a link to https://github.com/adswill/OnAir or https://on-air.dev.
- Refer to the project by name in articles, videos, reviews and bug reports, and use unmodified screenshots of OnAir for reviews or news.
- Redistribute unmodified OnAir releases under the name OnAir.
- Use the logo, unmodified, next to a truthful "based on OnAir" statement. It must not be the branding of your own project.

## Ask first

Please ask for written permission before you:

- name or brand a modified version, a derivative, a product or a company "OnAir", or with a name or logo close enough to be confused with it;
- use the OnAir logo as the logo, icon, packaging or site design of your own project, or change or recolour the logo;
- suggest that OnAir or its author endorses, sponsors or is affiliated with you or your product, or present your build as the official one.

A modified build should say that it is modified and who maintains it.

## How to ask

Open an issue at https://github.com/adswill/OnAir/issues or write via https://on-air.dev. Say what you want to use, where and for what.

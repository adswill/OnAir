jaero_c8400_48k.s8
  14 seconds (46 s to 60 s) of samples/8400bps_ambe_sample.ogg from https://github.com/jontio/JAERO (MIT, Copyright (c) 2015-2019 Jonathan Olds):
  a real Inmarsat Aero C channel (8400 bit/s OQPSK voice channel) as the 48 kHz audio of an SSB receiver, scaled and stored as signed 8-bit
  samples (the signal's carrier sits at about 2.5 kHz). Used by tests/aero/test_aero_cchan_real.cpp. Redistributed with the MIT notice; credit in THIRD_PARTY.md.
